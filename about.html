About Me

Hi, I’m Aaron.

I’m a Yale graduate from Florida. I studied Mechanical Engineering and Economics, and I enjoy work that combines people, hardware, planning, and problem solving.

Background

I’ve spent a lot of time around student teams, prototypes, and test hardware.

At Yale, I worked on launch vehicle structures, liquid rocket hardware, test stands, control systems, and mechanical prototypes.

Those projects brought together CAD, machining, analysis, purchasing, documentation, testing, lab setup, and safety planning.

I also led student teams, managed budgets, coordinated workspace needs, and helped turn large projects into smaller tasks with clear owners.

What I like working on

What keeps me going

I lift, run, and play bass guitar — three things that have very little in common except that they all reward showing up consistently. Lifting is the most directly measurable; the bar either moves or it doesn't. Running is slower feedback, which is useful. Bass is the odd one out — there's no finish line, just the gap between where you are and the music you're trying to play.

All three have taught me something about the difference between good practice and just putting in time. They're also a useful counterweight to engineering work, which can feel very abstract until something catches fire or breaks, or both.

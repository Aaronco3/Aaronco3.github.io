Responsible Engineer

Test Carriage

I owned the test carriage design. The frame used 8020 aluminum extrusion and was designed for horizontal engine testing with flexibility for future vertical configurations.

  • Designed for 5,000 lbf continuous axial thrust loading.
  • Used stainless steel 5/16-18 screws and custom gusset plates.
  • Accounted for shear and bending loads through frame joints.
  • Integrated engine mounting and fluid-system access into the carriage layout.
  • Prioritized low-friction thrust transfer into the load cell for cleaner data.

Testing

Data collection and test readiness

The support hardware was designed to make the engine, tanks, feed system, electronics, shielding, and thrust measurement hardware accessible during integration and testing.

  • Designed thrust path into a load cell for engine performance data.
  • Reduced friction losses in the carriage to improve thrust measurement quality.
  • Maintained access to fluid interfaces for assembly and troubleshooting.
  • Supported electronics integration on the GSE cart.
  • Included shielding requirements around the fuel tank.
  • Balanced modularity, mobility, safety, and integration access.

Subassemblies

Design targets I managed

Tank Mounting

Resist 200 lbf with a factor of safety of 2 while maintaining a physical barrier between fuel and oxidizer sources.

GSE Cart

Support accessible fluid and electronics integration, shrapnel-resistant fuel tank shielding, modularity, and mobility.

Test Carriage

Resist 5,000 lbf with a factor of safety of 5 while measuring thrust load and minimizing friction losses in the thrust path.

Responsible Engineer

Test Carriage

I owned the test carriage design. The frame used 8020 aluminum extrusion and was designed for horizontal engine testing with flexibility for future vertical configurations.

  • Designed for 5,000 lbf continuous axial thrust loading.
  • Validated the carriage structure with ANSYS finite element analysis.
  • Used stainless steel 5/16-18 screws and custom gusset plates.
  • Accounted for shear and bending loads through frame joints.
  • Integrated engine mounting and fluid-system access into the carriage layout.
  • Prioritized low-friction thrust transfer into the load cell for cleaner data.

Validation

FEA and pressure testing

Validation was split by subsystem. The test carriage was checked structurally in ANSYS FEA, while the fluids system was validated through pressure testing before engine test operations.

  • Validated the test carriage with ANSYS FEA before fabrication.
  • Designed the thrust path into a load cell for engine performance data.
  • Used pressure testing to validate fluids-system assembly.
  • Maintained access to valves, fittings, sensors, and load-path hardware.

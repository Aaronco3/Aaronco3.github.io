Project Liquid

Launch Vehicle

Full liquid-fueled rocket vehicle targeting FAR-OUT competition, June 2026.

Project Initiative

In Progress — Manufacturing & Validation

To design, manufacture, and launch a liquid-fueled launch vehicle to compete at the annual Friends of Amateur Rocketry Oxidizers Unlimited Tournament (FAR-OUT) in June 2026.

Structures — Subteam Lead, Spring 2025

Upper Airframe

Constructed from in-house manufactured G10 fiberglass — chosen for its high strength and RF signal transparency, enabling external communication with the GPS locator during recovery. Bulkheads are machined from 1/8" aluminum to withstand parachute loads.

The nosecone is fiberglass, sized to support a 2U cubesat payload in compliance with competition regulations. A 4:5 Von Karman Ogive profile optimizes aerodynamic performance.

Lower Airframe

Constructed from aluminum tubes and bulkheads, designed to resist internal pressures of the fuel and oxidizer tanks, and to serve as detachable clamshells for servicing fluid components as needed.

Avionics Bay — Responsible Engineer, Spring 2025

The avionics bay consists of an internal fiberglass body compressed between two welded bulk plates using internally threaded rods, to which the avionics mounting plates are attached. It serves as the coupler between two external body tubes, compressing a 1" switch band. Two ports equalize the internal pressure with the external environment, allowing the altimeter to correctly trigger parachute deployment.

Design Drivers

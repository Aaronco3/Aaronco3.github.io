Floe Actuated Manifolds

Outdoor manifold systems for controlled rooftop deicing.

Overview

I worked on two manifold variants that route deicing fluid through rooftop snow-melt systems: a three-valve model for zone control and a one-valve model for simpler installations.

Product Variants

Three-Valve Manifold

Routes fluid to three controlled outputs for installations that need separate roof zones.

One-Valve Manifold

Uses the same control approach in a lower-complexity package for single-zone routing.

My Role

System Integration

Packaged valves, tubing, fittings, and enclosure hardware into compact assemblies controlled from a central wired box.

Manufacturing Handoff

Created EBOMs, MBOMs, and SOPs to support repeatable assembly and production planning.

Airtable Tools

Built kit development tools that estimated product weight and tracked inventory across parts and assemblies.

Validation

Testing focused on valve cycling and sprayer selection. I ran 40 sprayer trials over five days across several brands, sprayer types, and tubing options to identify the setup with the best coverage in the target zone.

The sprayer testing compared range and coverage across different arc angles, with the final choice based on compatibility with the manifold product and installation requirements.

Project Summary

This project connected product design, test planning, and manufacturing support. The result was a clearer path from early manifold concepts to buildable, documented product variants.

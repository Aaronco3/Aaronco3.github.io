Spark Igniter Test Stand

Designed and built the standalone test stand for an augmented spark igniter. My work focused on the frame, blast shielding, mounting, plumbing layout, and test-readiness of the setup.

Test stand design Fluid systems Propulsion testing Machining
8020 frame mobile structure for igniter, tankside plumbing, shielding, and controls
Blast shield operator-side protection between controls and firing hardware
Fluid routing valves, regulators, fittings, and tubing packaged for inspection
Hot-fire setup standalone firing configuration before engine integration

Technical Achievements

  • Designed the test stand layout. Packaged the igniter, blast shield, fluid controls, and support hardware onto a compact mobile 8020 frame.
  • Built the mounting structure. Created a rigid firing-end support for the igniter while keeping the plumbing and electrical leads accessible.
  • Integrated the fluid system. Routed valves, regulators, needle valves, fittings, and tubing across the stand for standalone firing tests.
  • Machined test hardware. Manufactured copper and mounting components used at the igniter interface.
  • Prepared the stand for hot-fire testing. Supported setup checks, leak/fit inspections, and outdoor firing configuration.

Build Work

  • Frame: assembled the aluminum extrusion structure and mounting plates.
  • Shielding: positioned the blast shield between the firing hardware and operator-side components.
  • Plumbing: mounted flow-control hardware so fittings and tubing could be checked before each firing.
  • Igniter interface: machined and fit hardware around the copper igniter body and test outlet.

Data Collection + Test Readiness

Repeatable setup

Fixed mounting points kept the igniter position consistent across tests.

Visible flow path

Regulators, valves, tubing, and fittings were exposed for pre-test inspection.

Remote-friendly layout

The firing end was separated from the control side by the blast shield.

Ignition verification

The stand allowed standalone firing before installing igniter hardware into the engine system.

Photo/video records

Hot-fire footage was used to confirm flame behavior and document test outcomes.

Service access

The layout left room to adjust fittings, wiring, valves, and the igniter mount between firings.

Outcome

  • Completed a dedicated standalone platform for augmented spark igniter testing.
  • Enabled safer outdoor firing with shielding, fixed mounting, and separated controls.
  • Improved troubleshooting access for plumbing, wiring, and igniter-side hardware.
  • Provided a test configuration the team could use before engine-level integration.
